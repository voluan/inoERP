# NURA (Fujifilm): global footprint, operating model, country deployments, local partners and software (as of 4 Oct 2026)

Labels: **[C]** means confirmed by the cited source. **[I]** means inferred by the researcher. **[U]** means reported but unverified or conflicting. Fujifilm's fiscal year (FY) runs April to March, so FY2025 is Apr 2025 to Mar 2026 and FY2030 is Apr 2030 to Mar 2031.

## Q1. All NURA centers worldwide as of October 2026 (country, city, opening date, operator/partner, ownership)

### Takeaway
By about October 2026 there are roughly 12 to 15 operating NURA or NURA-derived sites in 5 countries: India (6 fixed centers plus 1 mobile bus), Mongolia (2), Vietnam (3 campuses), Thailand (1, opened 30 Jul 2026) and the UAE (the "PURA" sister center in Abu Dhabi, plus a directly operated Dubai center whose opening is not confirmed). Manila, Kuala Lumpur and Cape Town were announced for FY2025. I found no confirmation that any of the three had opened by Oct 2026. Fujifilm itself said 12 sites as of Jan 2026.

- [I] The 10-facility count of Mar 2025 most likely breaks down as India 5 + NURA Express 1 + Mongolia 2 + Vietnam 1 + PURA Abu Dhabi 1. Fujifilm appears to count PURA when it says it "operates in the UAE" before any Dubai NURA had opened.
- [I] The rise from 10 to 12 by Jan 2026 is most likely HCMC (Nov 2025) plus one of Hanoi Campus 2 or the Dubai center.
- [I] Openings after Jan 2026 that are confirmed: Chennai (May 2026) and Bangkok (Jul 2026). Best estimate for early Oct 2026 is about 14 operating sites, about 15 if Dubai is open. That is well behind a straight-line path to 100 by FY2030.
- [I] Bangkok opened in FY2026, not FY2025 as announced, so the ASEAN direct-operation rollout is running at least about 4 months late. Manila and KL may be similarly delayed.
- [I] FUJIFILM DKH is the operating platform for the whole network. It runs India directly, supports partners in Mongolia and Vietnam, receives Vietnamese data for AI support, and is the parent brand of the Thai entity "FUJIFILM DKH (Thailand) Co., Ltd.".

## Q2. NURA in Vietnam: location, partner, licenses, packages and prices, software and regulatory integrations

### Takeaway
Yes, NURA operates in Vietnam. It is run by a Vietnamese partner, Công ty CP Công nghệ Y tế Nhật Việt (VJH/JVH, MST 0110439304, chaired by Nguyễn Huy Tuấn, owner of T-Matsuoka Medical Center), with Fujifilm/FUJIFILM DKH supplying equipment, AI and know-how. There are three campuses (Hanoi ×2, HCMC ×1) and a public target of 10 centers by 2030. The only concrete software evidence is from NURA Vietnam's privacy policy: Fujifilm DKH LLP in India receives data for AI imaging support, with cross-border transfer to India, and the operator runs its own app and digital records. No local HIS, LIS or e-invoice vendor, and no social health insurance (BHYT) integration, is publicly named.

### Inferences
- [I] **License:** the "phòng khám đa khoa" label implies a general-clinic operating license from the provincial Health Department (Sở Y tế Hà Nội, and separately Sở Y tế TP.HCM for the HCMC campus) under the 2023 Law on Medical Examination and Treatment. I did not find license numbers.
- [I] **Software stack:** imaging AI, the screening workflow and the result/app platform are probably Fujifilm/FUJIFILM DKH's standard NURA IT system, shared across India, Mongolia and Vietnam. This rests on the "AI diagnostic imaging support" flow to Fujifilm DKH LLP India and the identical "medical IT system based on AI technology" wording in every PR. Local pieces would still be needed: Vietnamese e-invoicing (Decree 123/2020 and amendments), EMR compliance (Circular 46/2018, which Circular 13/2025 replaces), and data-protection filings under the PDPL 2025 and its decree. These are probably handled by local vendors or the T-Matsuoka group's existing systems. No vendor is publicly named. This is a gap and a possible opportunity.
- [I] **BHYT:** NURA is very likely self-pay or corporate-pay. Preventive check-ups are generally outside BHYT coverage, and no BHYT linkage is mentioned anywhere. The privacy policy names "insurance partners", which suggests private health insurers.
- [I] **Decree 13/2023:** NURA Vietnam's 2026 policy cites the PDPL 2025 and Decree 356/2025, not Decree 13/2023. That fits Decree 13/2023 having been superseded when the PDPL took effect on 1 Jan 2026 (background knowledge, not verified here).
- [I] **Price reconciliation:** 28,000 VND/day × 365 is about 10.2 million VND/year. The figures (6M VND/year in 2024, about 10M VND/year implied in 2025, 1M VND per JETRO) may describe different packages or tiers. Treat them as unreconciled.

## Q3. Fujifilm's stated targets for NURA in its medium-term plans

### Takeaway
The confirmed corporate target is **100 NURA locations by FY2030 (ending Mar 2031), mainly in emerging countries**, set in VISION2030 (Apr 2024) and repeated in later PRs. No public revenue or screening-volume target specific to NURA was found. A frequently repeated "30 locations by FY2026" figure is unverified, and a "JPY700bn by FY2030" figure attributed to the screening business is wrong: it belongs to Bio CDMO.

### Cited Findings
- VISION2030 briefing transcript (17 Apr 2024): "promote the overseas development of NURA… aim to open 100 locations by FY2030". Also: "expand our health screening center NURA to 100 sites around the world, mainly in emerging countries". — [VISION2030 briefing PDF](https://ir.fujifilm.com/en/investors/ir-materials/presentations/main/0117/teaserItems1/0/tableContents/0/multiFileUpload2_1/link/20240417_VISION2030_e_ts.pdf); [VISION2030 press release](https://holdings.fujifilm.com/en/news/list/1710)

- **Correction:** in the VISION2030 transcript, "JPY700 billion in FY2030" refers to Bio CDMO sales (20,000-litre tanks), not NURA or healthcare screening.   - The transcript gives no center count or revenue target.
  - Source: [Fujifilm Medical Systems Briefing, Apr 2026](https://ir.fujifilm.com/en/investors/ir-materials/presentations/session/main/0118/teaserItems1/0/tableContents/01116/multiFileUpload2_1/link/ff_presentation_20260408_004e.pdf)

### Inferences
- [I] To reach 100 sites by Mar 2031 from about 14 in Oct 2026, Fujifilm needs about 18 or more new sites per year. The actual pace has been about 3 to 4 per year (2021 to 2026), so the target implies a sharp step-up, probably through partner and turnkey deals (PURA-style, Tavan Bogd regional expansion) rather than direct operation alone.
- [I] Strategically, NURA works as a showcase and demand driver for Fujifilm CT, mammography and IT/AI sales, and as a data loop for AI development. Screening revenue alone is not the main stated rationale.

### Gaps

- "30 locations by FY2026" appeared only in a search-engine summary and could not be verified in the VISION2030 transcript.
- The Apr 2026 slides themselves were not machine-readable; only the transcript was extracted.

## Q4. Operating model: franchise, JV, Fujifilm-owned, or turnkey? Does Fujifilm provide the whole software package?

### Takeaway
NURA runs three models side by side:
1. **Fujifilm-led JV or direct operation** through FUJIFILM DKH: India; Thailand via FUJIFILM DKH (Thailand) Co., Ltd.; planned direct centers in Dubai, Manila and Kuala Lumpur.
2. **Partner-operated NURA-branded centers** with Fujifilm/FUJIFILM DKH support: Tavan Bogd in Mongolia, VJH/T-Matsuoka in Vietnam, The InUversal Group in South Africa.
3. **Co-branded or white-label "turnkey" centers** using NURA know-how: PURA, run by Pure Health in Abu Dhabi.

In every model Fujifilm supplies the devices plus an "AI-based medical IT system" and operational know-how. Remote reading and AI support are centralized (Kozhikode for India; data flows to India from Vietnam). Fujifilm does not describe the arrangement as a franchise or disclose its fees.

### Inferences
- [I] Fujifilm does provide the core clinical software to partners: imaging AI, a screening workflow or "medical IT system", remote reading, results and app experience, and training. This is consistent across all PRs and with Vietnam's data flow to Fujifilm DKH LLP India. Whether it also provides the back-office layer (billing, local e-invoicing, accounting, national-system integrations) is not stated. In partner markets it is probably localized by the partner.
- [I] The core NURA IT stack is probably built on Fujifilm's Synapse PACS and REiLI AI platform plus a NURA-specific workflow and app layer developed in India by FUJIFILM DKH. This rests on Fujifilm's portfolio and the India hub, not on any explicit statement.
- [I] Partner economics (equipment sale or lease, IT subscription, brand or royalty fee, remote-reading fee) are undisclosed. The "operational know-how + devices + IT" language suggests a turnkey or licensing package rather than a classic franchise.

### Gaps

- No information on country-specific integrations: India ABDM/ABHA, Mongolia e-health, Vietnam e-invoice, VNeID or the national EMR/health-data system.
